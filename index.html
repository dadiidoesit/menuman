Each path has a distinct obstacle and a unique reward at its terminus. All three paths converge at a junction guarded by gas spores (the room's resolution encounter). The party can split up or commit to a single path. ## Read-Aloud (Entry) *The tunnel opens into a low; wide cavern choked with fungal growth of a scale you have never seen. Shelf mushrooms the size of wagon wheels climb the walls in spiraling tiers. Clusters of luminous stalks - thin as fingers; tall as a man - sway gently despite the absence of any breeze. The floor is soft and yields under your boots like wet leather. A faint clicking sound echoes from deeper in; rhythmic and organic; like the mandibles of something patient.* *Three distinct paths wind through the fungal mass.* *To the left; a narrow trail between towering puffball formations; their surfaces taut and grey-white; straining like overripe fruit.* *Straight ahead; the wider central passage is completely sealed by a thick membrane of fungal tissue - translucent; faintly glowing; and pulsing gently. Something is visible through it but impossible to make out clearly.* *To the right; a crawl-space beneath a collapsed shelf of petrified mycelium. The gap is barely two feet high. Beyond it; you can hear the faint sound of running water.* --- ## LEFT PATH: The Puffball Corridor ### Obstacle The puffball mushrooms are pressurized with spore gas. Physical contact; loud noise; or vibration within 5 ft causes a puffball to rupture. Each ruptured puffball releases a 10 ft cloud of spores: - DC 12 Constitution save or poisoned for 1 hour (disadvantage on ability checks) - Fail by 5 or more: also blinded for 1 minute as spores coat the eyes - Moving at normal speed requires a DC 13 Stealth check per 30 ft of corridor - Combat in this corridor is extremely dangerous - any missed melee attack risks hitting a puffball (DM discretion; roughly 25% chance per miss) ### Traversal This is the stealth path. Rogues and characters with high Dexterity thrive here. The corridor is roughly 80 ft long. Three Stealth checks total to navigate safely. Failure on any check ruptures the nearest puffball. ### Reward: Lucky Fucker's Ring-on-a-Finger Necklace Found on a skeleton slumped against the wall at the corridor's end - a previous explorer who made it through the puffballs but never made it past the gas spores. Around the skeleton's neck: a leather cord threaded through a mummified finger still wearing a tarnished silver ring. **Lucky Fucker's Ring-on-a-Finger Necklace** *Wondrous Item; Uncommon* A severed finger (long since dried to jerky) wearing a simple silver band; hung on a leather cord as a necklace. The finger is a left index finger. The ring will not come off the finger. The finger will not come off the cord. You wear the whole thing or you wear none of it. - Once per long rest; when you fail a saving throw; you may reroll the die and take the second result. - Each time you use this ability; the finger twitches. - If you roll a natural 1 on the reroll; the finger points accusingly at you for the rest of the day. This has no mechanical effect but is deeply unsettling. *The previous owner was; by all evidence; extremely lucky right up until the moment they weren't.* --- ## MIDDLE PATH: The Fungal Membrane ### Obstacle The central passage is sealed by a living fungal membrane - a translucent wall of interlocking mycelium roughly 3 inches thick; spanning floor to ceiling and wall to wall. It is warm to the touch and pulses with a slow rhythm; like breathing. **Properties of the membrane:** - Resistant to slashing and piercing (the fibers are elastic and close around cuts) - Vulnerable to fire (it ignites readily; but burning it fills the corridor with toxic smoke - DC 13 Con save or 2d6 poison damage and blinded for 1 round) - Vulnerable to acid (dissolves a passage-sized hole in 1 round if a flask of acid is applied) - Can be forced through with DC 16 Strength (Athletics) - the membrane stretches like a rubber sheet; then tears; coating the creature in sticky residue (disadvantage on Dexterity checks for 10 minutes unless cleaned) - DC 14 Nature check reveals it's a fruiting body defense - the colony sealed this passage to protect something behind it. Cutting through carefully (DC 15 Survival; 10 minutes of work) opens a clean hole without smoke or residue. ### What's Visible Through the Membrane Squinting through the translucent tissue; characters can make out a faint glow on the other side - something small and bright sitting on a natural stone shelf. The membrane distorts it like looking through a fogged window. ### Traversal The membrane is the only obstacle on this path. Once past it; the corridor is clear and short - roughly 40 ft to the junction. ### Reward: Lucky Fucker's Summer Camp Anklet Sitting on a natural stone shelf just beyond the membrane - clearly visible through the tissue but unreachable without breaching it. A woven friendship bracelet; sized for an ankle; made of fraying colored thread with small clay beads spelling out letters. The beads spell "L-U-C-K-Y-F" on one side and nothing legible on the other (the remaining beads fell off long ago). **Lucky Fucker's Summer Camp Anklet** *Wondrous Item; Uncommon* A child's friendship anklet; clearly handmade; with mismatched thread and lopsided beads. Absolutely should not be magical. Is anyway. - While wearing this anklet; whenever you roll a natural 20 on an attack roll; you also regain 1d4 hit points. It's not much; but it feels great. - Once per long rest; you may declare "I learned this at camp" before making a Survival or Athletics check. You gain advantage on that check. You do not need to have attended camp. You do not need to explain what camp. - The anklet cannot be destroyed by nonmagical means. It can get dirty; wet; and gross; but it holds together. It's a friendship bracelet. It was made with love. *How it got sealed behind a living fungal membrane in the Underdark is a question the anklet declines to answer.* --- ## RIGHT PATH: The Crawl-Space ### Obstacle A collapsed shelf of petrified mycelium has created a gap roughly 2 feet high and 3 feet wide. The passage beyond is clear; but getting through requires squeezing. - **Small creatures:** Move through normally (no check required) - **Medium creatures:** Must squeeze. DC 12 Acrobatics to fit through without getting stuck. Failure means the creature is stuck and must spend an action to free themselves (DC 10 Athletics). Armor heavier than light armor imposes disadvantage on the Acrobatics check. - **Large creatures:** Cannot fit. Period. - The gap is roughly 10 ft long. Once through; the passage opens up again. ### Beyond the Gap A side chamber with a shallow stream - clean; cold; fresh water fed by the underground water system that connects to the waterfall cavern deeper in. This is the first drinkable water the party has found (they are nearly dead of thirst; this matters). However; the stream chamber is home to 2-3 **violet fungi** (MM p.138) rooted along the water's edge. They attack anything within their 10 ft reach. The chamber is cramped; making ranged combat difficult but not impossible. After the violet fungi are dealt with; the passage continues another 30 ft to the junction. ### Reward: Lucky Fucker's Poison Dart Kit (Now With 50% More Arsenic!) Wedged into a crack in the wall near the stream; wrapped in oilcloth that has somehow survived the damp: a small leather roll-up kit containing six darts; a vial of milky liquid; and a singed instruction card. **Lucky Fucker's Poison Dart Kit (Now With 50% More Arsenic!)** *Wondrous Item; Uncommon* A professional-looking dart kit in a leather roll; branded with a logo of a grinning skull giving a thumbs up. The instruction card reads: "LUCKY FUCKER'S PREMIUM POISON DART KIT - NOW WITH 50% MORE ARSENIC! For recreational use only. Lucky Fucker's Ltd. is not responsible for any deaths; dismemberments; international incidents; or hurt feelings resulting from use of this product. DO NOT LICK THE DARTS. WE CANNOT STRESS THIS ENOUGH." - Contains 6 poison darts (1d4 piercing; range 20/60; finesse; thrown) - On hit; target makes a DC 13 Constitution save. On failure; target takes 2d6 poison damage and is poisoned for 1 minute. On success; half damage; not poisoned. - Once all 6 darts are used; the vial of milky liquid can recoat 6 more darts (any darts; not just these). The vial contains enough for one recoating. - After both the darts and the recoating are used; the kit is just a nice leather roll. Which is still useful; honestly. *The 50% more arsenic claim has not been verified by any regulatory body.* --- ## THE JUNCTION: Gas Spore Resolution All three paths converge in a roughly circular chamber where the bioluminescence intensifies.
